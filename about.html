About Smart Nutrition System

Smart Nutrition System is a student project designed to help users explore food information and local food suggestions. Users can register, log in, browse food recommendations and record their daily meals.

🎯 Our Goal

To make general nutrition and food information simple and accessible.

📍 Local Food

To display sample food suggestions according to the user's locality.

📊 Food Tracking

To help users record meals and view simple food activity summaries.

Note: This is an educational project. Food suggestions are general information, not personal medical or dietary advice.
